Most triage systems look confident even when the evidence is weak.

Ticket Triage™ does something simpler: deterministic rules, visible reasons, and an explicit REVIEW state when the evidence is ambiguous.

Same input + same rules = same result. If the rules conflict or do not have enough evidence, it does not guess.
